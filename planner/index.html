Keyboard and mouse help
  • Arrow keys move the crosshair (hold Shift for bigger steps). Enter or Space places a point.
  • F finishes a shape. Backspace removes the last point. Escape cancels drawing.
  • Ctrl or Cmd + Z undoes. Ctrl or Cmd + Y, or Shift + Ctrl + Z, redoes.
  • Plus and minus zoom. 0 fits the image. Mouse wheel zooms; hold Shift and drag to pan.
  • Double-click finishes a shape when drawing with the mouse.

      Checks

        Coverage and anchors

        Finds how many anchors give every walkable cell at least 3 anchors in range with clear line of sight. A geometric simulation, not a radio guarantee.

        Required. Take it from your hardware datasheet or a site survey; the planner has no default.

        On the plan: red hatching = fewer than the required anchors; light teal = covered; diamonds = anchors.

        Crowd-aware planning

        Compares this plan with and without its crowd zones, using the range, grid, candidate and seed settings above. The search budget applies to each search (three are run). Crowds shorten usable range and raise the anchors needed; the coefficients below are planning assumptions, not measurements.

        Leave empty if every crowd zone already has a density. There is no default: you choose it.

        Crowd model coefficients

        Anchor placement optimizer

        Ranks candidate layouts for this plan by simulated positioning error (median and P90), and compares them with a naive grid of the same size. It uses the range, grid, spacing, mounting, seed and budget set above, and the fallback crowd density from the crowd section. The errors come from the stand-in simulator and the mock predictor, so they are not Traceline results.

        Export

        Files are saved to your computer. The image itself is not copied; keep the original beside plan.json.
